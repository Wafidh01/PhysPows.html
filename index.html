📐 Physics Formulas

🚀 Vector Resolution Formulas

  • • Horizontal Component: vₓ = v · cos(θ)
  • • Vertical Component: vᵧ = v · sin(θ) - g·t
  • • Resultant Velocity: v = √(vₓ² + vᵧ²)
  • • Angle: θ = tan⁻¹(vᵧ / vₓ)

🌀 Circular Motion Formulas

  • • Centripetal Force: F꜀ = (m · v²) / r
  • • Centripetal Accel.: a꜀ = v² / r
  • • Period: T = (2π · r) / v
